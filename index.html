THE IDEA BEHIND THE PAUSE

AI assists.
You decide.

Human Pause explores productive friction: a short, intentional interruption that invites you to examine an AI answer before using it.

THE WORKFLOW

Notice → Reflect → Verify

Local keyword rules flag health, finance, and scientific topics. A gate hides the answer and asks you to describe a verification step. A brief reflection unlocks the content.

The prototype checks length, word variety, and verification-related language. It cannot assess the quality or truth of your reasoning.

RESEARCH CONTEXT

Keeping human agency in the loop.

Built for the Misinformation and AI in Public Health project at the New York Academy of Sciences Junior Academy.

This is a concept prototype, not an evaluated intervention or a medical product. It does not prove a reduction in automation bias.

HONEST LIMITS

A checkpoint, not a guarantee.

Keywords can miss consequential answers and flag harmless ones. No source verification or AI fact-checking takes place. Completing a reflection does not certify an answer.

The extension depends on third-party page structure and may briefly expose streamed text before detection. It is not a security boundary.
